Our story

Built on a little stubbornness.
And a lot of thread.

We kept waiting for a marketplace that made room for independent designers, unusual ideas, and clothes with a little personality. Then we remembered: we own scissors. We can make things.

So we built House of Briar—a home for wearable art and the people behind it. Whether you’re making your first piece or finding your next favorite, pull up a chair. You don’t have to fit a mold here. We’d probably turn it into a hat anyway.

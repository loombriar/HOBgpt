Photo tips (optional)
Four useful views
  • Front: the whole piece, clearly lit—ideal for your cover.
  • Back: show the shape and closures.
  • Detail: fabric, stitching, texture, and any imperfections.
  • Fit: on a person, mannequin, or styled flat to show proportion.

Use a simple background and natural light. Keep colors true to the piece. These are suggestions, not four required uploads. Add left/right views for a fuller angle gallery.

JPEG, PNG, or WebP · up to 10 images · 10 MiB each · choose Make main photo on the image shoppers should see first, then save your listing. iPhone HEIC/HEIF photos must be exported as JPEG before upload.

Our story

Good taste.
Questionable restraint.

Fashion has enough rules. We brought scissors.

For the designers with wild ideas, overflowing sketchbooks, and absolutely no interest in playing it safe: this is your runway.

For the shoppers who want something nobody else at the party is wearing: welcome. We saved you the good stuff.

House of Briar brings the makers and the outfit repeat-offenders-in-recovery together. Original fashion, wearable art, and pieces that make strangers stop you on the street.

Because life's too short for boring clothes. And frankly, your closet deserves a plot twist.

How the House works

A marketplace built around independent designers.

When a piece sells, House of Briar retains a 10% marketplace fee from the designer’s sale and 90% stays with the designer. Buyers are not charged an extra 10% marketplace fee. The fee helps operate and maintain the marketplace, support orders, promote independent designers, and build tools for the creative businesses in the House.

Shipping and applicable taxes are shown separately where required.

Our story

Built on a little stubbornness.
And a lot of thread.

We kept waiting for a marketplace that made room for independent designers, unusual ideas, and clothes with a little personality. Then we remembered: we own scissors. We can make things.

So we built House of Briar—a home for wearable art and the people behind it. Whether you’re making your first piece or finding your next favorite, pull up a chair. You don’t have to fit a mold here. Around here, “a bit much” is a compliment.

Your pattern studio

Sell original sewing patterns as PDF downloads or paper patterns. These are sewing instructions and templates, not finished clothing.

  1. Create a listing in the Designer dashboard. Choose Sewing Patterns, then PDF downloads or Paper patterns.
  2. Show the finished design in your preview photos. Describe the included sizes, skill level, seam allowances, printing format, and permitted use. Choose “Custom / Other” for multi-size patterns and explain the range.
  3. For a PDF, save the draft, then upload its file below. For paper patterns, set your available stock and preparation time.
  4. Submit the listing for review. PDFs are delivered privately after verified payment; paper patterns are mailed with tracking.

Attach a PDF to a saved draft

Uploading a replacement returns a published PDF listing to draft for review. Previous buyers keep the file they purchased.

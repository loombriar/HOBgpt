Your pattern studio

Sell original paper sewing patterns. These are sewing instructions and templates, not finished clothing.

  1. Create a listing in the Designer dashboard. Choose Sewing Patterns, then Paper patterns.
  2. Show the finished design in your preview photos. Describe the included sizes, skill level, seam allowances, and permitted use. Choose “Custom / Other” for multi-size patterns and explain the range.
  3. Set your available stock and preparation time.
  4. Submit the listing for review. Mail purchased paper patterns with tracking.

Privacy Policy

Updated October 9, 2026

House of Briar operates the House of Briar marketplace website and related Android application. For privacy questions, access, correction, or deletion requests, contact houseofbriar26@gmail.com.

Information we process

Depending on how you use the service, we process your name, email address, account identifiers, sign-in verification codes and sessions, designer profiles, listing details and uploaded photographs, favorites, messages and inquiries, order and shipment details, support requests, and records of purchases, refunds, donations, and seller payouts. If you voluntarily submit measurements for a fit request, those measurements and related messages may be shared with the relevant designer. Measurements saved only in your browser remain on that device until cleared.

We process basic site activity, including anonymous session identifiers and approximate country, region and city inferred from IP address. We do not intentionally store precise location coordinates for this analytics feature. Server and security infrastructure may process IP addresses and technical diagnostics separately.

Why we use it

We use this information to authenticate accounts, operate listings and orders, communicate with buyers and designers, provide support, process payments and payouts, prevent abuse and fraud, maintain security, improve reliability, and meet accounting and legal requirements. Optional marketing communications should be sent only where you have opted in or another lawful basis applies.

Who receives information

Relevant order and fit-request details may be shared with the designer fulfilling your request. Payment processors, hosting, email-delivery, identity or login providers, and shipping-related providers may process data needed to deliver their services. Stripe and any enabled PayPal checkout handle payment information under their own policies. We do not need your full card number to process an order. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or to protect the service and its users.

Android permissions and device data

The Android app displays the House of Briar service. When you choose to upload photographs, the Android system or browser may offer a photo picker or file chooser. Select only images you intend to share. Device permissions and picker behavior depend on the installed app version and Android version; permissions and background behavior must be validated on a physical release device before publication. We do not claim to need continuous location, microphone, or contact access for normal shopping.

Storage, retention and security

We use access controls, authenticated sessions and encrypted network connections. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Account information and favorites may be removed when you delete an eligible customer account. Order, payment, payout, refund, fraud-prevention, dispute and legally required records may need to be retained for an appropriate period, and access to them is restricted as applicable. We do not publish a universal deletion deadline because the applicable retention period depends on the record and legal obligation.

Your choices and account deletion

Customers can sign in and open the Customer Account dialog to choose Delete account permanently. For security, a recently verified sign-in and explicit confirmation are required. Accounts with linked transactions or messages may be blocked from automatic deletion pending review. Designers, and customers needing assistance with a blocked deletion, can request account closure and personal-data review by emailing houseofbriar26@gmail.com. We may verify your identity and retain information necessary for pending orders, payouts, disputes or legal compliance. Removing the app from your phone does not delete your account. You may also clear locally stored measurements and preferences in your browser or device settings.

Children

The marketplace is intended for adults aged 18 and over; we do not knowingly offer accounts to children.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the service changes and will post the revised date. For questions or privacy rights requests, email houseofbriar26@gmail.com. See also our Marketplace Rules.

Request deletion of your House of Briar account

This page is publicly accessible; you do not need to sign in to read the instructions or request assistance. It applies to both customer and designer accounts on the House of Briar website and Android app.

Delete your account in the app or website

  1. Customers: Sign in to House of Briar, open Customer Account, then select Delete account permanently.
  2. Designers: Sign in to Designer Studio, open Settings, then select Delete designer account.
  3. For your security, sign in again when prompted and confirm the deletion request.

Request deletion without signing in

If you cannot access your account, or deletion is blocked because of orders, subscriptions, connected payouts, or shared records, email thee.house.of.briar@gmail.com with the subject House of Briar account deletion request. Tell us whether you have a customer or designer account and the email address associated with it. Do not send passwords, sign-in codes, payment card details, or identity documents by email. We may need to verify account ownership before processing a request.

What is deleted and what may be retained

Eligible account deletions remove the account and sign-in sessions, along with associated saved preferences, profiles, favorites, and other personal data that can safely be erased. Designer listings, uploaded images, and shared records may require additional review. Transaction, payment, payout, refund, fraud-prevention, dispute, and legally required records may be retained for applicable legal, accounting, or security obligations. Some requests therefore require manual review rather than immediate deletion.

Deleting the Android app does not delete your House of Briar account. For further details about information collected, retention, and your rights, read our Privacy Policy.

Shipping & Delivery

House of Briar is a marketplace of independent designers. Shipping cost and delivery timing can vary by piece and designer. We show the applicable cost before payment so it does not arrive as a checkout surprise.

Shipping cost

Available delivery options and their exact cost are shown before you submit payment. Product and cart pages should surface known shipping information as early as possible.

Delivery estimates

Each piece may have its own preparation time. Delivery estimates should distinguish the designer’s handling or made-to-order time from the carrier’s transit time. Tracking is provided when available after shipment.

Free shipping

Free-shipping offers can vary by piece, designer, or promotion. When an order qualifies, the applicable threshold or terms are shown with the product or before checkout.

International orders

International availability depends on the piece, designer, destination, and carrier support. Duties, taxes, customs charges, and import restrictions may apply and should be disclosed before purchase whenever they can be determined.

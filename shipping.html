Free US delivery

Delivery is included in the listed price. There is no extra delivery charge at checkout.

When will my order arrive?

Check the preparation time on each piece. That is how long the designer needs before shipping. The carrier’s delivery time comes afterward.

Buying from more than one designer?

Each designer ships their own pieces. Your order may arrive in separate packages on different days.

Tracking and help

Signed-in customers can find submitted tracking in their account. For help, email houseofbriar26@gmail.com with your order reference. Guest shoppers should keep their receipt.

Checkout currently accepts US addresses only. Delivery estimates are not guaranteed dates. Existing paid orders keep their original totals.

Shipping and returns policy

For designers

You pay postage and packaging. Include those costs when setting your price. The marketplace fee still applies to the sale price; the House does not reimburse postage or automatically buy shipping labels for you.

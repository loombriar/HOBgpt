From the Briar to your door

Shipping & Delivery

Your pieces come from independent designers. Each designer prepares and ships their own pieces, so a suitcase can arrive in separate packages.

Where we ship

Online checkout currently accepts United States delivery addresses only. A designer’s interest in international orders does not make international checkout available.

What shipping costs

Each piece has a flat US shipping price, charged for each unit purchased. $0 means free shipping. If the designer has not set a price, that piece cannot be purchased yet. Your suitcase shows charges grouped by designer and the combined total before payment.

When a piece has a free-shipping threshold, it counts the merchandise from that designer before discounts. Gift wrapping, shipping and other designers’ pieces do not count. Meeting the threshold waives shipping only for pieces carrying that offer.

Preparation and delivery

Preparation or handling time is the designer’s time before handing a package to the carrier. Made-to-order pieces may take longer. Carrier transit comes afterward. If preparation time is missing, ask the designer before ordering. Estimates are not guaranteed delivery dates; checkout does not offer express or overnight delivery.

Your address and tracking

Stripe collects your delivery address. House of Briar stores it and shares it with the designers fulfilling your order. Card details stay with Stripe. Signed-in shoppers can see submitted tracking in their account. Tracking updates depend on the carrier and tracking service.

Taxes and order help

Automatic tax calculation is not enabled in this checkout. Shipping prices do not establish whether a purchase is tax-exempt.

For delays, missing packages, returns and other order concerns, read the House rules and shipping terms and return and refund terms, or contact House of Briar with your order reference. Avoid sending card information by email.

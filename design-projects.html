Agree the details together

Offers are introductions, not bookings or payments. Before work begins, agree the quote, materials, fittings, timeline, delivery and revision arrangements. Accepting an offer shares both account emails and the project details with the chosen maker; it removes the project from the board. Uploaded images are resized for viewing.
